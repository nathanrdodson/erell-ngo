What makes this different

Typography

Space Grotesk + Space Mono

Space Grotesk has slightly irregular letterforms — the 'a', 'e', 'g' have character that DM Sans doesn't. It reads as designed, not generated. Space Mono appears for all scientific/data elements: coordinates, species labels, field notes. The contrast between the two gives the site a distinctive register.

Layout

Left-aligned, not centered

The hero is bottom-left aligned — title stacks large at the left edge, mission text and CTAs sit in a row below. No centered-everything hero. The pillars use an asymmetric grid (2fr 1.5fr 1.5fr). Ghost numbers behind each card in the background. The quote section is left-aligned too.

Color + Specificity

Ochre statement + real science

One golden ochre section (#B4873A) breaks the cream/green pattern — completely unexpected, signals a real design decision. The species strip uses real Erell study species in italicized Latin: Uta stansburiana, Crotaphytus collaris. Coordinates appear in the hero. Green stays in the footer.
